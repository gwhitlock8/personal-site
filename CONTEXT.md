# Gavin's job search site

A public site that presents Gavin to hiring teams, plus a private admin area where Gavin tracks his own job search.

## Public site

**Visitor**:
Anyone using the public site, usually a hiring manager, founder or recruiter.
_Avoid_: User, guest

**Lead**:
A Visitor who left their contact details on the public site.
_Avoid_: Contact, prospect

**Fit Check**:
A comparison of one job description against Gavin's background, listing matches and gaps.
_Avoid_: Match score, JD analysis

**Case Study**:
A short public write-up of one piece of Gavin's work: the problem, what he built, and the outcome.
_Avoid_: Project, portfolio item

## Job search (admin)

**Application**:
Gavin's candidacy for one role at one company, created from an ATS confirmation email or added by hand.
_Avoid_: Job, candidacy, submission

**Opportunity**:
A role a recruiter or company raised with Gavin before he applied; it becomes an Application if he pursues it.
_Avoid_: Lead, inbound, prospect

**Stage**:
Where an Application stands: Applied, Recruiter Screen, Hiring Manager, Technical, Demo, Final or Offer, or one of the end states Rejected, Withdrawn or Accepted.
_Avoid_: Status, step

**Event**:
One dated thing that happened on an Application or Opportunity, such as an email received or sent, or a note.
_Avoid_: Activity, update, touch

**Proposed Change**:
A change to an Application's Stage or details that the assistant suggests from an Event and that only takes effect when Gavin approves it.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, auto-update

**Follow-up**:
An Application or Opportunity that has gone too long without an Event, so Gavin should act on it.
_Avoid_: Reminder, stale item, nudge

**Watchlist**:
The companies whose job boards are checked for new roles.
_Avoid_: Target list, company list

**Aggregator**:
A third-party list of open roles across many companies, such as a recruiting agency's job board or the monthly Hacker News "Who is hiring?" thread.
_Avoid_: Feed, source, job site

**Job Match**:
An open role found on a Watchlist board or Aggregator that fits Gavin's search criteria: an SE or presales title, individual contributor, in Austin or remote anywhere in the US. Marking it applied creates an Application; one he passes on is marked Not interested. A Job Match whose posting disappears is Closed.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, recommendation, posting

**Digest**:
The weekday-morning email summarizing follow-ups due, Proposed Changes to review and new Job Matches.
_Avoid_: Reminder email, report
